# SANAA — Concept & Landing Page Brief

## 1. What SANAA is
A digital platform and creative marketplace that showcases, connects and promotes African creative talent to a wider audience.

- **Name:** *Sanaa*, Swahili for art / artistry.
- **Core belief:** Africa has incredible creative talent, but unequal access to visibility, opportunity and platforms.
- **Positioning:** A digital home where African creativity meets visibility, discovery and opportunity.
- **Core message:** African creativity deserves to be seen, discovered and experienced.
- **Tagline:** *A continent of creativity. An infinite canvas.*
- **What it is primarily:** A creative ecosystem of portfolios, opportunities, collaborations, community and learning. An immersive digital exhibition, **not** another job marketplace.

## 2. The problem
The gap isn't talent. It's the gap between **talent, visibility and opportunity**.

- **Creative side** (an illustrator in Nairobi): posts on Instagram and gets likes, but clients can't find her, she has no professional showcase, doesn't know where paid work is, and competes with millions of posts.
- **Client side** (a business in Lagos): needs an African illustrator for branding and has no idea where to look.
- **SANAA brings the two together.**

Reference platforms: NewComma (talent + paid opportunities), Unpublished Africa (visibility + visual storytelling), Ananse (African creative commerce + storytelling).

## 3. Audiences
| Creatives | Opportunity seekers |
|---|---|
| Graphic designers, illustrators, photographers, fashion designers, 3D artists, animators, writers, filmmakers | Startups, businesses, creative agencies, brands, event organisers, individuals |

## 4. User journey (example: Amina, Kenyan photographer)
1. **Create a profile:** registers and builds her creative profile.
2. **Showcase:** uploads photos, projects and descriptions.
3. **Get discovered:** clients browse profiles.
4. **Find opportunities:** projects, collaborations, commissions.
5. **Connect:** a client reaches out about a project.

Client side: browse → filter by discipline → contact the right creative.

## 5. Four pillars
| Pillar | Value |
|---|---|
| **Showcase** | Give your work visibility |
| **Discover** | Find inspiring creatives and projects |
| **Connect** | Build meaningful creative relationships |
| **Grow** | Find opportunities to develop your craft |

## 6. Art direction
- **Avoid:** green/yellow/red palettes and generic "tribal" patterns.
- **Aim for:** contemporary African creativity with an editorial, gallery-inspired, collage-driven visual language.
- **Philosophy:** celebrate creative expression and don't impose one visual style. **The work is the star.**
- **Colours:** cream `#FDF2E0`, ink `#111111`, lime `#D5F244`, tangerine `#FF6A13`, lilac `#A982F5`, sun `#FFD54A`.
- **Type:** heavy condensed uppercase sans (Archivo) for headlines, bold serif (Fraunces) for section headings, Inter for body, brush script (Permanent Marker) for expressive moments.
- **Graphic language:** collage imagery, tilted cards, brushstroke underlines, the four-tile mark.

## 7. Logo (Option A: Brush × Wall)
- **Mark:** four tilted tiles in a 2×2 grid: tangerine, lime, lilac and an ink circle (cream circle on dark). Represents the Creative Wall.
- **Wordmark:** "sanaa" in Archivo, weight 900, width 82, tight tracking, converted to outlines.
- **Brushstroke:** tapered tangerine stroke under the wordmark, the artist's hand.
- **Versions:** full logo (cream / dark), mark only, app icon, social avatar, favicon (tiles straightened for 16–64 px).
- **Rules:** clear space of one tile width on every side; minimum 120 px wide (full logo), 24 px (mark); use on cream or ink only; don't recolour, stretch or re-tilt the tiles; don't drop the brushstroke from the full logo.

## 9. Interactions
| Interaction | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Creative Wall | Hover or focus reveals discipline, title, creator and *View project* (always visible on touch screens) |
| Discipline filters | Highlight works by discipline; non-matching works fade but stay in place |
| Discipline cards | Filter the wall and jump to it |
| Project preview | Click any work to open a larger view; Escape or the close button closes it |
| Chapter links | Jump to each chapter of the wall |
| Animations | Floating hero cards and looping pillar illustrations; off for reduced-motion users |

## 10. UX principles
- **Clear pathways:** instantly obvious it serves both creatives and clients.
- **Discoverability:** explore disciplines without page-hopping.
- **Accessibility:** readable contrast; colour never the only cue; keyboard-accessible controls; descriptive alt text; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Responsive:** the wall is re-composed for tablet (3 columns) and phone (2 columns), not just shrunk; swipeable rows on phones.
- **Clear CTAs:** every section earns its place and guides toward an action.
